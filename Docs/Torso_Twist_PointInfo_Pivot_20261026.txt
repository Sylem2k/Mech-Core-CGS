OCTOBER 26, 2026 - MECH TORSO POINTINFO PIVOT

I noticed that the CGS_TorsoTwistComponent only exposed m_vPivot as numeric local coordinates. I could not grab the pivot with a PointInfo gizmo in Workbench to decide exactly where the torso starts turning.

I added the editable m_TorsoPivotPoint PointInfo to CGS_TorsoTwistComponent. I initialize it against the mech ROOT and read its model-local position once in OnPostInit before the system can remove the obsolete root visual. I use that single local point for all three existing twist calculations: desired aim yaw, the Torso/Cockpit/arm mount transformation, and root-mounted weapon yaw/pitch aiming. It changes the rotation ORIGIN; it does not rotate the mech legs, root entity or whole vehicle.

I retained the old m_vPivot field as a fallback. This preserves my unusual legacy mech configurations when they have not been assigned a PointInfo. Adding a PointInfo overrides the fallback. A workbench prefab needs to be respawned after moving its gizmo because the pivot is read once on spawn.

I preconfigured a real m_TorsoPivotPoint PointInfo on all 25 standard production mechs: 10 standard player frames and 15 regular OPFOR mechs. Each starts with Offset 0 0 0 to match its former default. I can now open any of those mechs, select CGS_TorsoTwistComponent > Twist > Torso Pivot Point, and drag the green/blue/red PointInfo gizmo to the intended waist joint (usually centered between the hips at the rotation seam). The value is unique to each prefab, so a light, medium, heavy, or assault can have its own pivot.

I did NOT modify Titan_WarLord_OPFOR, Titan_WarLord_BOSS_128_Test or the two old root-only prototype bases. The boss has an existing nonzero numeric pivot (-0.2166 12.339 7.5671), bespoke hitboxes and unusual visuals. It retains the fallback until its body split and collision are validated. New prefabs with CGS_TorsoTwistComponent can manually add m_TorsoPivotPoint.
