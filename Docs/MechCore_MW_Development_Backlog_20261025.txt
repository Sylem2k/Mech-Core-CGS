MECH CORE CGS + MECHWARRIOR (MW) - DEVELOPMENT BACKLOG
Recorded: 2026-10-25
Status: OPEN / UNVERIFIED. These are my observations and requests, not confirmed root causes.
Repositories: Mech-Core-CGS and Reforger-Warfare--MechWarriorProject-main

PURPOSE
I am collecting the multiplayer, controls, customization, and presentation problems I want fixed.
I will keep shared MechCore changes separate from MW-specific implementation. I do not want
unrelated code changes or existing Titan boss balancing reverted during these fixes.

1. MENU / UI FLASHES WHILE PLAYING
   I see the menu/UI flash whenever I change weapons, go to the next screen, or perform other
   movements. I need to determine which UI is being recreated or made visible and stop the
   unintended flashing. Confirm that legitimate HUD changes and menus still work.
   Likely investigation: MechCore HUD/input plus MW MechLab/combat HUD when applicable.

2. MULTIPLAYER JUMP PACK RUBBER-BANDING
   In multiplayer, when I activate the mech jump pack/jet and jump, I get snapped back to
   the position where I started. I need the server-authoritative movement and client
   prediction/correction investigated. This must be tested with a remote client, not only
   local Workbench. I want all players to see the same jump position and landing.
   Likely investigation: BFA_SentinelJumpJetComponent and movement replication.

3. CONFIGURABLE WEAPON GROUPS / NUMBER-KEY SELECTION
   Players have asked me for a way to assign individual weapons or weapon types to whichever
   weapon group they want, then press 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (and more where supported) to select
   those groups. I want a loadout/configuration UI, proper server-side weapon-group routing,
   working hotkeys, and consistent replication. A per-weapon assignment should be unambiguous;
   selection must not fire unintended modules or interfere with infantry keybinds.
   Likely investigation: MechCore crew station/router plus MW MechLab and weapon modules.

4. MECH BAY - TWO PLAYERS / WRONG OWNERSHIP
   When two players stand near the mech bay, both can see the mech being built, and the
   system struggles to determine who owns the build. I need builder sessions assigned to
   the specific interacting player rather than any nearby player. Only the intended builder
   should control the build and receive the finished mech unless I intentionally implement
   shared builds. Test two clients standing next to the same bay and using it concurrently.
   Likely investigation: MW RW_MechBayComponent, RW_MechLabMenu, saved builds, ownership/RPL.

5. FOLLOW ME - MECHS FOLLOW THE WRONG PLAYER
   My Follow Me order is causing mechs to follow another player rather than the person
   who issued the command or owns the mech. I need the follow target assigned and maintained
   per mech/per issuing player on the server and replicated consistently. Test two owners,
   several mechs, switching orders, disconnect/reconnect, and no cross-follow.
   Likely investigation: CGS_MechRadioFollowAction and CGS_MechCompanionOrderAction.

6. MULTIPLAYER LASERS / WEAPON FX / RPL AUDIT
   My lasers are not showing to other players in multiplayer. I want replication reviewed
   for ALL weapon families, not just lasers: visibility, projectile/tracer/beam effects,
   firing sounds, impacts, damage authority, module switching, and late joiners.
   The server must own gameplay damage; remote clients must see correct effects without
   duplicate or missing projectiles.
   Likely investigation: CGS/MechCore weapon FX and MW laser/pulse/PPC/ballistic/missile code.

7. COCKPIT VIEW
   I want to design a cockpit camera/view for mechs. I need to establish a configurable
   interior camera point, appropriate cockpit mesh/HUD, visibility of the pilot, correct
   seat ownership, and first-person/third-person transitions, including multiplayer.
   This is a feature/design task; it is not implemented merely by adding an exterior camera.

8. PILOT VISIBILITY / SEAT ATTACHMENT IN MULTIPLAYER
   Other players see pilots sitting on TOP of their mechs, but when a person drives their
   own mech the driver appears invisible. I need occupants attached to the right physical
   or visual seat position for remote clients, while preserving the pilot's expected camera
   and vehicle-control behavior. Test both player viewpoints simultaneously.
   Likely investigation: seat transforms, compartment manager, visibility flags and RPL.

9. REMOTE MECH MOVEMENT IS BLOCKY / TELEPORTING
   When watching other players drive mechs over the network, their mechs look like they
   lag-jump between positions instead of moving smoothly. I need smoothing/interpolation
   and movement replication studied without giving up server authority. Test two clients,
   packet delay and moderate packet loss, stepping/turning, and fast movement.
   Likely investigation: BFA_MechMovementComponent, BFA_MechMovementSystem, replication.

10. MECH FOOTSTEPS TOO LOUD / NEED DISTINCT SOUNDS
    The current mech footsteps are much too loud. I want per-mech sound sets so light,
    medium, heavy and assault mechs (or specific chassis) can sound different. I need
    sensible attenuation, volume, cadence, surface response and multiplayer playback,
    without repeated or doubled footstep sounds.
    Likely investigation: core procedural leg/step sound events, chassis sound configs.
