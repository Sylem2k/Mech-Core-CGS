OCTOBER 26, 2026 — COCKPIT FIRST-PERSON BODY-ONLY FIX

MY GOAL
I want all standard mechs to be built with their exterior body as a distinct Torso part and their future cockpit interior as a separate Cockpit part. In FIRST PERSON inside the mech, only the exterior Torso body should disappear. The arms, legs, weapons, scenery, HUD and an installed cockpit must remain visible. In THIRD PERSON and outside, I want the complete mech visible to myself and everyone else. I do not want to make the entire mech invisible again.

THE BUG I FOUND
The previous pilot visibility pass recursively hid every mech child visual and removed the mech root XOB while a player occupied it. That incorrectly hid legs, arms, weapons and the body in third-person too. My screenshot with Gravemarch-G shows the body disappearing even though I am looking at the mech from outside in third-person.

MY FIX
I replaced the broad recursive hiding with a surgical local-client operation. BFA_MechControllerComponent finds a prefab's separate exterior Torso slot and toggles EntityFlags.VISIBLE only on that one attached body entity. The local character's CameraHandlerComponent.IsInThirdPerson() / CharacterControllerComponent.IsInThirdPersonView() determines which view I am using. On changing to third-person, exiting or changing seats, I restore the torso's visual flag. The code never touches the mech root model, limb visuals or mounted weapons. Unknown camera state defaults to showing the body, not hiding the entire mech. This remains local: other clients and the dedicated server are not instructed to hide anything.

I preserved the separate CGS_MechCockpitInteriorComponent marker for imported interior geometry. In CGS_TorsoTwistComponent I automatically capture a populated Cockpit slot beside the configured Torso slot so the interior follows the upper-body yaw twist. I do not double-add the slot if it was explicitly configured.

STANDARD MECH BUILD RULE
- Separate exterior Torso entity attached to a named Torso slot.
- Separate interior Cockpit entity attached to a named Cockpit slot. The slot is empty until I model the interior.
- Legs, arms, weapon mounts and hardpoint modules remain separate parts and are never hidden by cockpit logic.
- The established torso-root visual split (m_bHideRootMesh = 1) stays in place for standard frames; it exists to support body twisting, not pilot camera visibility.
- A modeled cockpit receives CGS_MechCockpitInteriorComponent and stays physically separate from the exterior body XOB.

PREFABS PREPARED
I added an empty Cockpit slot (no placeholder prefab and no physics merge) to all 10 player frames and 15 normal OPFOR variants which already have Torso slots. That gives 25 production mech prefabs the same Torso/Cockpit layout without changing their existing weapon/physics definitions.

SPECIAL CASES / NOT YET CONVERTED
I did not alter the playable-root architecture of the two old Core prototype bases (Test_Mech_Base, New_Test_Base) or the special Titan_WarLord_OPFOR and Titan_WarLord_BOSS_128_Test boss meshes. They still use a root body without a dedicated separate Torso slot. I must split those bodies into visual torso prefabs and validate collisions / the Warlord's 17 special damage hitboxes before claiming they follow the same first-person rule. Do not accidentally hide boss hitboxes or compromise shields.
