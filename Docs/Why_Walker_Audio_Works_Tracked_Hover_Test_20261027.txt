OCTOBER 27, 2026 — WHY WALKER AUDIO WORKS BUT TRACKED/HOVER AUDIO IS SILENT

I confirmed that the normal walker system works, and tracked/hover movement itself is functional. I do NOT need to rewrite the movement physics. This controlled test is meant to isolate the sound fault before I copy another vehicle audio library.

ROOT DIFFERENCE
My walkers trigger SOUND_MECH_STEP and SOUND_MECH_SERVO directly from BFA_MechLegComponent at the swing/plant transitions. The leg part is the audio source. Its SOUND_MECH_STEP maps to the known-working {8B7D7D9A72F30BAD}Sounds/SampleMech.acp.

The tracked/hover frame instead has a separate CGS_MechDriveAudioComponent on the root. It runs a frame tick, checks locomotion mode, measures speed and yaw, searches the root SlotManagerComponent for Track_L1/Track_R1 or HoverPad_L1/HoverPad_R1, checks that part's SoundComponent and SCR_SoundDataComponent, and requests its event. These are additional possible failure points.

IMPORTANT BUG IN PREVIOUS DIAGNOSTICS
The helper SCR_SoundManagerModule.CreateAndPlayAudioSource returns void and can silently skip the sound. It may be unable to create an audio source because AudioSystem.IsAudible returned a negative value or the sound project did not resolve. My previous PLAY_REQUESTED status meant only the call was made, NOT that an actual sound source existed.

HOW I TEST
1. I back up my functioning project folders first and load BOTH matching diagnostic branches (Core + MW). CGS-MW-Assets stays on main.
2. In Workbench I compile Game scripts, rebuild audio resources, then load Light or Assault tracked/hover frame nearby.
3. I look for [CGS DRIVE AUDIO INIT] and [CGS DRIVE AUDIO FIRST_FRAME] on the listening client. Missing INIT means the new component or new files are not loaded. INIT but no FIRST_FRAME means the audio frame tick isn't running. FIRST_FRAME followed by mode mismatch means the wrong mode.
4. I should hear exactly one known-good walker stomp from the track/hover part when it starts its audio test; this is a test sound, NOT a permanent mech audio design.
5. If the walker stomp IS audible but the track/hover engine sounds aren't, the core event path is working; next I will fix the new ACP mixer, event/shader, bank attenuation or audio samples.
6. If there is NO stomp, I use the exact status logs to distinguish missing attached sound-emitter entity or event mapping from an actual source-creation failure.
7. SOURCE_CREATED_PLAY_SUBMITTED still does not prove that human ears heard sound. I must independently listen and confirm the expected event.
8. I remove the walker test sound mapping and turn off m_bDebugSound once the actual track/hover sounds work. I do not merge this debug branch into main.
