TORSO TWIST - UPPER BODY AND WEAPON FOLLOW
October 25, 2026
My MechCore / MW development notes (first person)

I found that having CGS_TorsoTwistComponent on every mech was not enough. Most frame and enemy prefabs had EMPTY m_aTwistSlots / m_aTwistMounts, and their visible upper-body MeshObject was still on the vehicle root. Their leg movement and body visual therefore remained tied together, and many weapons were installed directly at root hardpoints instead of inheriting the torso movement.

I made a test-branch-only change in Mech Core CGS:
- CGS_TorsoTwistComponent now automatically discovers the MechWeaponMountComponent's arm/weapon mount IDs, even when the prefab's explicit twist-mount list is empty.
- Aim-mount weapons still use their proper independent yaw/pitch and match the twisted mount rest pose.
- I added tracking of ROOT-mounted weapon module entities at hardpoints (torso, chest, shoulders, back, any fire group), and rotate their existing local transform around the waist pivot. Root guns no longer remain fixed to the leg-facing chassis.
- I skip modules mounted under aim mounts or explicit bones so they do NOT receive the torso yaw twice.
- I also discover fresh weapons when the mech is refitted during an active twist, and detect late-bound arm mounts with a throttled 0.25-second scan.
- No fire-group changes, weapon damage, aim-range limits, leg simulation, pilot control or shield changes were made in this pass.

I made a companion test-branch-only MW prefab change:
- I added six reusable torso-visual entity prefabs with correct .et.meta resources, covering light, medium and Warlord model and material variants.
- I updated ten player/legacy frame prefabs (Light_Mech_Base, Light_Mech_Frame_B, Med_Mech_Base, Med_Mech_Frame_B, Heavy_Judiciar_Gun, Heavy_Judiciar_Missile, Heavy_War_Lord, Assault_Gravemarch_Gun, Assault_Gravemarch_Missile, SampleMech).
- I updated all 15 non-boss OPFOR variants (four Cricket, four Warden, four Judiciar and three Gravemarch).
- These prefabs now attach the matching visual upper-body model to a Torso slot, list the Torso and applicable exhaust/head slots as twist parts, and set m_bHideRootMesh=1. Legs and hips stay on their original slots. The original root MeshObject resource and physics, damage, and weapon configs remain serialized/unchanged for safety; the split torso is the rendered body while active.
- In the frame where SampleMech had no torso component, I added one.
- All 25 revised mech prefabs retain their original filenames and resource GUIDs. Newly added torso-visual prefabs use their own new GUIDs and metadata.

IMPORTANT BOSS EXCEPTION:
I deliberately did NOT relocate the active Titan Warlord / 128-player boss body mesh or any of its custom 17 hitboxes. Its existing torso-component and weapon mounts can use the new shared Core mount/root weapon-follow code, but a complete visual torso split on this boss still needs a separate hitbox-alignment and performance test. Its 40,000 hull / 30,000 shield / 18 hardpoints / virtual AI stations and prior damage fixes were not edited in the MW boss prefabs by this pass.
