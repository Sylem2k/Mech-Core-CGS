WHAT I CHANGED
I added only ONE NEW Core gameplay script:
  Scripts/Game/CGS_Movement/CGS_MechDriveAudioComponent.c

I install this component ON THE MECH ROOT once per tracked/hover chassis, rather than on every wheel/tread. It reads the MODE from the known-working BFA_MechMovementComponent, gets locally observed root displacement and yaw over elapsed time, and triggers appropriate sound events. It then resolves the part mounted in Track_L1 or HoverPad_L1 using the root SlotManagerComponent; if the left part is missing, it tries the R1 part.

The playback call is the SAME stable pattern used by normal BFA_MechLegComponent:
  SCR_SoundManagerModule.CreateAndPlayAudioSource(emitter, eventName)
Here emitter is the attached track/hover PART, which has a SoundComponent and SCR_SoundDataComponent. The audio component prevents duplicate playback by selecting just one part per vehicle, even on Assault frames with four track or hover parts. Sound effects are generated locally on listeners, without per-sound network RPCs.

EVENTS / MOVEMENT
Tracked, while translating or pivoting:
- SOUND_MECH_TRACK_CLANK approximately every 0.7 s
- SOUND_MECH_TRACK_RUMBLE approximately every 3.85 s (sample-based rumble, NOT a native seamless loop)
Hover:
- SOUND_MECH_HOVER_IDLE sample retriggered approximately every 3.85 s
- SOUND_MECH_HOVER_IDLE_PULSE approximately every 3.4 s while stationary
- SOUND_MECH_HOVER_THRUST approximately every 1.1 s while translating or pivoting

Movement detection uses local root displacement and yaw divided by elapsed time, not a frame-count-dependent distance threshold. The controller samples at most about 7 times per second. It can ignore far-away vehicles to avoid unnecessary sound processing; actual audible range is also governed by the audio project.
